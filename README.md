##  Project Overview

This project analyzes a Global Superstore Sales dataset to uncover valuable business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior, profitability, shipping performance, and market trends.

The project demonstrates the complete Data Analytics workflow, including Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Feature Engineering, Business Insights, and Data Visualization using Python.

---

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze overall business performance.
- Identify top-performing products and customers.
- Compare sales and profit across categories and markets.
- Understand the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Analyze shipping performance.
- Discover seasonal sales trends.
- Generate business recommendations based on data.

---

- Records: 51,290
- Columns: 21

##  Key Business Insights

- Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
- Furniture generated high sales but comparatively lower profit.
- Discounts negatively affected profitability.
- November and December recorded peak sales.
- Shipping Cost showed a strong positive correlation with Sales.
- A small number of customers generated a large percentage of total revenue.
- Delivery performance remained consistent across shipping modes.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in Technology products.
- Optimize discount strategy for low-margin products.
- Improve profitability of Furniture category.
- Strengthen customer loyalty programs.
- Focus marketing campaigns during peak sales months.
- Monitor loss-making products regularly.
- Improve sales performance in low-performing regions.
- Optimize shipping costs without affecting delivery performance.
